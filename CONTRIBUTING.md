# Contributing to Chronolox Specifications

We welcome community engagement, academic review, and technical critiques of the Chronolox architecture and security model.

## Scope of Contributions

- **Clarity & Completeness:** Identifying ambiguous specifications, missing threat vectors, or edge cases.
- **Cryptographic Models:** Evaluating our hardware-enforced TEE model, drand threshold fallback, and AEAD calldata architecture.
- **Typo & Translation Fixes:** Minor edits and corrections.

## How to Submit

1. For security vulnerabilities, do NOT use public GitHub issues or PRs. Email `security@chronolox.com`.
2. For questions and suggestions, open an [issue](https://github.com/Chronolox/specs/issues).
3. For concrete specification improvements, open a [pull request](https://github.com/Chronolox/specs/pulls).
